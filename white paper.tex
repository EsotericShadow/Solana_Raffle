\section{The Problem: Centralized Raffles Are a Scam}
Centralized raffle platforms, such as \href{https://degendraw.vercel.app/}{Degendraw}, fail the crypto community:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Centralized Control}: Platforms manage raffles, sidelining users from creative or financial control.
    \item \textbf{Opaque Operations}: Degendraw’s lack of active raffles and vague mechanics raises trust concerns.
    \item \textbf{Limited Features}: No user-generated content or community incentives, resulting in a stale experience.
    \item \textbf{Regulatory Risks}: Mixing fiat or physical prizes invites legal scrutiny, hindering innovation.
\end{itemize}
Degens need a transparent, decentralized platform that maximizes profit and engagement.

\section{The Solution: Solana Raffle Decentralized Dream}
Solana Raffle addresses these issues with a robust, user-centric design:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{User-Created Raffles}: For a 0.05 SOL fee, users create raffles and keep 90\% of ticket sales (post-initial phase).
    \item \textbf{Secure Escrow}: Funds are locked in a smart contract, ensuring payouts or refunds.
    \item \textbf{Monthly Mega Raffle}: Every ticket enters a free monthly draw, funded by 20\% of fees.
    \item \textbf{Crypto-Only Prizes}: SOL, tokens, and NFTs avoid regulatory issues.
    \item \textbf{Skill-Based Trivia}: A crypto quiz adds fun and legal clarity.
\end{itemize}
Unlike Degendraw’s minimal setup, Solana Raffle is a dynamic ecosystem driven by its community.

\section{Technical Architecture: Built to Moon}
Solana Raffle leverages Solana’s high-performance blockchain:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Blockchain}: Solana, with 65,000 TPS and low fees, outpaces Base’s Ethereum L2.
    \item \textbf{Smart Contracts}: Rust with Anchor, adapted from \href{https://github.com/kobby-pentangeli/solana-escrow}{open-source escrow} and \href{https://github.com/microgift/nft-raffle}{raffle logic}.

    \item \textbf{Frontend}: React with Tailwind CSS, hosted on Vercel.
    \item \textbf{Meme Coin Swaps}: Jupiter API for token conversions.
    \item \textbf{Escrow}: Trustless fund management for SOL and NFTs.
\end{itemize}
